Deliver the **working result**. Do not narrate how long a clip lasts, how many degrees per second, or
which of three options I could take — decide, apply, and report what now works.

**Why:** said directly on 2026-09-19, after several turns where I presented measured numbers and asked
him to pick between variants: *"мне все вот эти твои изъяснения, что это длится столько, это мне все не
нужно… Ты сам думай и решай… Я всё равно в этом ничего сейчас не вижу и не понимаю. Ты мне давай
результат в конце."* He is not reading the numbers; presenting them shifts my work onto him.

**How to apply:**
- When a tuning value is needed (turn rate, threshold, blend time), pick the one that gives the best
  result and set it. Do not offer variants A/B/C.
- Report in two or three lines: what now works, and the one thing left for him if any.
- Keep the measurements — they are still how I verify — just do not put them in the reply unless he asks
  why something behaves a certain way, or unless a claim of mine turned out wrong and he needs to know.
- Still stop for genuine design decisions (what the feature should DO) and for anything destructive.
  This is about tuning and mechanics, not about direction.

Exception that still stands: when I got something wrong, say so plainly — see
[[feedback-verify-never-presume]] and [[feedback-stop-the-patch-loop]].
