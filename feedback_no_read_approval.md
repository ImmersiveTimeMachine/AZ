Never ask for permission/approval to read files, search, glob, grep, or explore the codebase. Just do it.

**Why:** User has explicitly told me multiple times to stop asking for read approvals. It wastes time and interrupts workflow.

**How to apply:** Always proceed directly with Read, Glob, Grep, Agent (Explore), and any other read-only operations without asking first. This applies to all files in all projects on the machine.
