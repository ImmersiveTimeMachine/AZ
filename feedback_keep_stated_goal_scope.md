When Artur states what to concentrate on, build the plan or ТЗ only around that goal. A prerequisite I discover goes into the plan only as a dependency or risk line, plus a separate document or task. It does not become the main part.

**Why:** on 2026-10-16 he said: "fixing DX12 is a separate complex task, file it as a ТЗ; we concentrate only on the DX12 integration". I wrote a ТЗ where DX12 stabilisation was the main part. He corrected me: "я уже говорил, что не надо сосредотачиваться на стабилизации… наша цель интеграция". Here the goal was NoesisGUI on DX12.

**How to apply:**
- Before writing a plan, restate his goal in one line and check every section against it.
- An extra problem found along the way goes into the plan as a dependency or risk, with a pointer to a separate document or task.
- When his message is voice-garbled ("директив 12" = DirectX 12, "ТТ" = ТЗ), read it in the context of the current goal. Do not reinterpret the goal.
